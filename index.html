Fire in the engine bay?

How heat, fuel and electrical faults can come together under the bonnet.

Read article about engine bay fires

The engine bay packs hot exhaust parts, fuel lines and wiring into a tight space. A fuel leak near the exhaust or damaged wiring can start a fire. When a mechanic traces an electrical fault, they look for frayed insulation, damaged connectors and wiring that has rubbed against metal.

Metal in the gearbox oil?

What those shiny flakes can tell you about the condition of your gearbox.

Read article about metal in gearbox oil

A light paste on a magnetic drain plug can come from normal wear. Visible flakes, chips or pieces of teeth are worth showing a mechanic, especially if the gearbox is noisy or has started shifting poorly.

Ready for a long drive?

A quick check before you head out can save you a stop along the way.

Read article about preparing for a long drive

Check cold tyre pressures against the handbook or door sticker. Make sure the lights work, top up the screenwash and check that the wipers clear the glass. Check the oil and coolant according to your handbook before setting off.
